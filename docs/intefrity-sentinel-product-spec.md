# Integrity Sentinel
## AI-Powered Enterprise Integrity Intelligence

**Continuous detection of fraud, collusion, abuse, insider risk, and operational anomalies through behavioral analytics, relationship intelligence, and AI-driven investigations.**

---

# Platform Narrative

Integrity Sentinel is a conceptual enterprise intelligence platform designed to help organizations continuously monitor operational integrity across people, workflows, approvals, vendors, transactions, and operational systems.

Inspired by modern operational intelligence platforms and built for the era of AI-driven investigations, Integrity Sentinel combines:

- graph analytics
- behavioral modeling
- explainable AI
- anomaly detection
- relationship intelligence
- operational investigation workflows

into a unified enterprise platform.

The platform vision focuses on helping organizations move from periodic auditing and reactive investigations toward continuous integrity monitoring and operational trust intelligence.

Most organizations still rely on:

- fragmented data silos
- spreadsheet-based investigations
- reactive audits
- quarterly compliance reviews
- disconnected dashboards
- manual fraud analysis

As organizations scale, hidden operational patterns become increasingly difficult to detect:

- approval collusion
- procurement fraud
- policy circumvention
- insider abuse
- suspicious vendor relationships
- anomalous operational behavior
- coordinated manipulation
- governance drift

Traditional BI systems surface metrics.

Integrity Sentinel is designed to surface relationships, intent, behavioral anomalies, and operational risk.

---

# Core Platform Capabilities

## Enterprise Integrity Graph

At the center of the platform is the Enterprise Integrity Graph.

Operational objects become entities:

- employees
- managers
- vendors
- approvals
- reimbursements
- nominations
- departments
- devices
- transactions
- documents

Relationships between entities are continuously modeled and analyzed:

- approved_by
- submitted_by
- reports_to
- related_to
- shares_ip_with
- transferred_to
- frequently_interacts_with

This enables detection of hidden operational patterns and relationship anomalies.

---

## Behavioral Risk Intelligence

Integrity Sentinel establishes behavioral baselines across enterprise activity.

The platform continuously analyzes:

- approval behavior
- spending trends
- nomination patterns
- communication relationships
- access patterns
- workflow anomalies
- timing irregularities
- semantic similarity

Potential risks are surfaced through explainable scoring models.

### Illustrative Example

```text
Risk Score: 87/100

Contributing Factors:
- 92% semantic similarity with prior submissions
- Shared approval network detected
- Elevated after-hours activity
- Cross-department coordination anomaly
- Unusual submission velocity
```

- produce executive summaries
